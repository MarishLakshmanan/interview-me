# Interview Me

A practice tool where a candidate holds a live voice interview with an AI interviewer tailored to a specific job, then receives a written critique of how they did.

## Language

**Candidate**:
The person practising, who answers the interviewer's questions by voice.
_Avoid_: User, interviewee

**Interviewer**:
The AI voice persona that asks the questions and holds the conversation.
_Avoid_: Agent, bot, assistant

**Briefing**:
The material the candidate supplies before an interview: the job description, the company summary, and their resume.
_Avoid_: Context, inputs, setup

**Interview**:
One live voice conversation between the candidate and the interviewer, from its opening to its wrap-up.
_Avoid_: Session, call, chat

**Question plan**:
The ordered set of questions prepared from the briefing before the interview starts.
_Avoid_: Question set, script

**Planned question**:
One entry in the question plan: the question to ask, what it tests, and what a strong answer would cover.
_Avoid_: Prompt, item

**Follow-up**:
An unplanned question the interviewer asks in response to an answer, belonging to the planned question that prompted it.
_Avoid_: Probe, sub-question

**Assessment**:
The written report produced after an interview, saying for each question what was right, what went wrong, and how to improve.
_Avoid_: Feedback, evaluation, review, score
